# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary users are overseas procurement managers, EPC teams, and regional distributors evaluating YKE as a power equipment supplier for export projects. A secondary audience is YKE's internal Chinese-speaking sales and technical team, who use the Chinese helper pages for review, coordination, and partner communication.

## Product Purpose

YKE's website exists to help overseas buyers and channel partners qualify the company quickly, understand which product lines fit their projects, review credible delivery proof, and start a quotation or distributor conversation with confidence. Success means the site feels export-ready, technically reliable, and easy to act on.

## Brand Personality

Steady, technical, responsive.

The site should feel trustworthy, competent, and commercially mature. It should project the confidence of an experienced export manufacturer without turning into a dark, macho industrial poster site.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS-style layouts with shallow trust signals and interchangeable card grids
- Beige editorial minimalism that hides industrial substance behind design tropes
- Overly dark, aggressive heavy-industry sites that feel dramatic but unhelpful to buyers
- Marketing-first pages that make visitors hunt for specs, delivery proof, or contact paths

## Design Principles

- Show proof before claims: certifications, delivery records, standards, and support process should do the persuasion.
- Organize by buyer decisions: product fit, project evidence, export capability, and response path should appear in that order.
- Keep English primary and polished: Chinese support should be complete, but the main commercial flow should remain export-first.
- Make technical content feel navigable: dense information is good when it is structured, scannable, and purposeful.
- Every page should end with a clear next step: quotation, consultation, or partnership inquiry should never feel buried.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site should meet WCAG AA contrast expectations, preserve visible keyboard focus states, support reduced motion, and keep bilingual layouts readable on desktop and mobile. Important actions and trust signals should remain understandable without relying only on color or animation.
